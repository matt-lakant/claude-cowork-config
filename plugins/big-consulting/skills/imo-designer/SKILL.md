# Integration Management Office Designer

## What this produces
A one-page IMO charter, a workstream structure, a governance and decision-rights design, pre-close rules, and a staffing estimate.

## Inputs to ask for
- The deal thesis and value targets
- Org charts for both companies
- Expected signing-to-close timeline and regulatory approvals pending
- Leaders available to staff workstreams
If value targets are not set, build the structure and flag value capture as the first IMO deliverable.

## Method
1. Choose an integration approach per function from the thesis: absorb into buyer, take the best of both, keep standalone, or transform both.
2. Define workstreams: functional (finance, HR, IT, legal, operations, sales) plus cross-cutting (Day 1, value capture, communications, culture). Name a lead from each company where possible.
3. Design governance: steering committee (biweekly), IMO (weekly or daily near close), workstreams (weekly). For each forum: members, inputs, and what it decides.
4. Write decision rights: what a workstream decides alone, what goes to the IMO, what goes to the steering committee.
5. Set pre-close rules with counsel: until close the companies operate independently, competitively sensitive data goes only to a clean team, and no coordination of pricing, customers, or employees.
6. Standardize artifacts: charter, milestone plan, value tracker, risk and issue log, interdependency log, weekly status.
7. Staff it: a full-time IMO lead, estimated hours per workstream lead, and the exit criteria for handing work back to line management.

## Output format
- IMO charter (one page)
- Table: Workstream | Scope | Leads | Integration approach | First milestone
- Table: Forum | Members | Cadence | Decides | Inputs
- Decision-rights matrix, pre-close rules, staffing table

## Quality checks
- Every value target maps to a workstream owner
- Pre-close rules are marked for counsel review
- Every forum has a stated decision scope

## Notes from the source article
- **Replaces:** Standing up the integration management office between signing and close: charter, workstreams, governance cadence, templates, and the escalation path.
- **Example request:** “We signed on the logistics acquisition last week and expect close in 90 days. Design the IMO: workstreams, governance, and who decides what.”
- **What the user still owns:** Putting your best people on it. The design needs leaders with real authority and time, and pulling them off the business is a cost only you can decide to pay.
- When handing the deliverable back, name in one line the decision above that stays with the user.
