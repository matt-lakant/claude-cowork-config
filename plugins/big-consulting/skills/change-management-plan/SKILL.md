# Change Management Plan

## What this produces
A sponsor and change-agent roster, an ADKAR plan per affected group, a training plan, a resistance plan, and an adoption scorecard.

## Inputs to ask for
- The change impact assessment or a description of affected groups and impacts.
- Go-live date and major milestones.
- Named sponsor and available managers or change agents.
- If no impact assessment exists, run a light one first and label it.

## Method
1. Define the sponsor role: visible actions, dates, and messages. Sponsors are the senior leaders with authority over the affected groups.
2. Recruit change agents at about 1 per 25 to 50 affected employees, drawn from respected frontline staff.
3. For each high-impact group, plan actions against Prosci's ADKAR model: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement. Each stage gets an action, owner, and date.
4. Build training by role: format, hours, timing (within two weeks of go-live), and proficiency check.
5. Anticipate resistance per group (loss of status, workload, skill fear) with a specific response and owner.
6. Define adoption metrics: usage, proficiency, and outcome measures, with targets at 30, 60, and 90 days.

## Output format
1. Roster: Role | Name or Position | Responsibilities.
2. ADKAR plan: Group | Stage | Action | Owner | Date.
3. Training plan: Role | Format | Hours | Timing | Proficiency Check.
4. Resistance plan: Group | Likely Resistance | Response | Owner.
5. Adoption scorecard: Metric | 30 | 60 | 90 Day Target.

## Quality checks
- Every high-impact group has actions at all five ADKAR stages.
- Metrics measure behavior and outcomes, beyond training completion.
- Reinforcement continues at least 90 days after go-live.

## Notes from the source article
- **Replaces:** The change workstream in a transformation, when a team builds the sponsor plan, training, resistance management, and adoption metrics around a go-live.
- **Example request:** “Here’s the impact assessment for our service center consolidation. Build the change plan, with who does what at each stage and how we’ll know it’s working.”
- **What the user still owns:** Showing up as the sponsor. No plan survives an executive who skips the town halls.
- When handing the deliverable back, name in one line the decision above that stays with the user.
