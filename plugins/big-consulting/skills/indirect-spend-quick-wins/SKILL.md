# Indirect Spend Quick-Win Finder

## What this produces
A ranked quick-win list with savings, time to cash, effort, and next action, in 30, 60, and 90-day waves.

## Inputs to ask for
- Classified spend (spend cube output) or AP lines for indirect categories
- Contract list with renewal dates, notice periods, and auto-renew terms
- Software list: seats purchased, seats assigned, seats active in the last 90 days
- Item-level purchase history across sites
If license usage is missing, ask IT for admin-console exports for the top ten applications.

## Method
1. Licenses: purchased minus active seats, times price per seat. Target reductions at the next true-up or renewal.
2. Renewals: list contracts renewing in the next 180 days whose notice window has not closed. Flag each for renegotiate, right-size, or cancel.
3. Price harmonization: the same item or rate paid at different prices across sites. Savings = volume x (price paid minus lowest current price).
4. Consolidation: more than three suppliers for the same need. Estimate savings as a labeled range.
5. Tail: suppliers under USD 10K per year. Route to catalog or card.
6. Score each win: annual savings, days to first saving, effort (low, medium, high), supplier relationship risk.
7. A quick win delivers inside 90 days with no RFP. Everything else goes to the sourcing pipeline.

## Output format
- Table: Win | Category | Supplier | Annual savings | Days to first saving | Effort | Risk | Next action | Owner
- Waves: 30, 60, 90 days with totals
- Sourcing pipeline list (items that need a full event)

## Quality checks
- Every savings figure shows its formula and inputs
- Renewals with closed notice windows are excluded or flagged
- License savings count only seats that can be cut at a known date
- No item appears in two wins

## Notes from the source article
- **Replaces:** The first 90 days of an indirect procurement program, banking savings before any sourcing event runs.
- **Example request:** “Here’s our spend cube, contracts, and SaaS seat report. Find savings we can bank this quarter without an RFP.”
- **What the user still owns:** Calling the vendors. Savings arrive only when someone with authority makes the renewal call before the notice window closes.
- When handing the deliverable back, name in one line the decision above that stays with the user.
