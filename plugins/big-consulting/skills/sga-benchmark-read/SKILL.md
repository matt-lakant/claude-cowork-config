# SG&A Benchmark Read

## What this produces
A functional benchmark table and a short read: where you sit, what the gap is worth, and how much is addressable.

## Inputs to ask for
- Your P&L with SG&A split by function, plus headcount and contractors by function
- Revenue, business model, countries operated, recent acquisitions
- A peer list of 5 to 12 public companies, with their 10-K or annual report pages attached
Never supply benchmark figures from memory. With no peer data attached, deliver only the internal normalization.

## Method
1. Map your cost to one functional taxonomy: Finance, HR, IT, Legal, Procurement, Marketing, Sales, Executive, Facilities. Load fully: salaries, benefits, contractors, outsourcing, systems.
2. Compute: SG&A % of revenue, each function % of revenue, FTEs per USD 1B of revenue, IT spend % of revenue.
3. Normalize peers: note whether their SG&A includes R&D, depreciation, stock comp, or one-time charges, and adjust or footnote.
4. Take the peer set as given (build and defend it with a peer benchmark set builder first if none exists); the work here is the functional cost mapping. Flag any peer outside roughly 0.5x to 2x your revenue for scale effects.
5. Compute peer median and top quartile. Under 5 peers, label ranges directional.
6. Gap = (your ratio minus peer ratio) x your revenue, at median and at top quartile.
7. For each gap, test structural explanations first: direct sales versus channel, regulated industry, multi-country footprint, unintegrated acquisitions, deliberate growth spend.

## Output format
- Table: Function | Your cost | % revenue | Peer median | Top quartile | Gap $ at median | Structural share | Addressable gap | Deep-dive hypothesis
- Peer notes: company | source page | adjustments made
- Five-sentence read for the CEO

## Quality checks
- Every peer number cites an attached source page
- Definitions are reconciled before any gap is computed
- Addressable gap excludes the structural share
- No gap is called a savings target

## Notes from the source article
- **Replaces:** The benchmarking workstream that lines up SG&A by function against peers and turns the gaps into a savings range.
- **Example request:** “Here are our functional costs and 10-Ks for eight peers. Where is our G&A really out of line?”
- **What the user still owns:** Picking the peers. Management picks flattering ones and investors pick harsh ones; decide which argument you are preparing for.
