# Steering Committee Readout

## What this produces
A 4 to 6 page readout: decisions, status, milestones, escalations, and the action log, with detail in an appendix.

## Inputs to ask for
- Workstream updates, including each lead's self-reported status
- Baseline plan with milestone dates (attach the plan or tracker)
- Budget versus actual
- Benefits or KPI tracker
- RAID log (risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies)
- Decisions requested and actions from the last meeting
If no status thresholds exist, use the defaults in step 2 and state them on the status page.

## Method
1. Page 1 is decisions: each with options, recommendation, date needed, and the cost of delay in time or money.
2. Recompute status per workstream from data. Default thresholds: Green, on baseline dates and within budget. Amber, forecast slip of 2 weeks or less or up to 5% over budget, with a recovery plan. Red, anything beyond that, or no recovery plan. Show where the recomputed color differs from the self-reported one.
3. Show trend: last period's color next to this period's, with the reason for every change.
4. Watermelon check: flag any workstream reported green while milestones slip, open issues grow, or high risks age past 30 days.
5. Escalations: only issues outside the program team's authority, each with a specific ask of a named committee member.
6. Milestones for the next 60 days: baseline date, forecast date, variance.
7. Financials and benefits: spend against budget, benefits tracked against the business case.
8. Action log from the last meeting: closed or open, owner, date.

## Output format
- Page 1 table: Decision | Options | Recommendation | Needed by | Cost of delay
- Page 2 table: Workstream | Last | This period | Self-reported | Reason | Recovery action
- Milestone table, escalation list, financial summary, action log
- Appendix: workstream detail

## Quality checks
- Every color follows the stated thresholds
- Every color change has a reason
- Every decision has a deadline
- Every action from the last meeting is accounted for

## Notes from the source article
- **Replaces:** The weekly or monthly steering committee pack a program team assembles from workstream updates, where too much effort goes into making every workstream look green.
- **Example request:** “Here are the six workstream updates and the tracker for the billing system program. Build Thursday’s steering committee readout and tell me where the greens aren’t really green.”
- **What the user still owns:** Calling red when it is red. The skill recomputes the colors; saying so to the sponsor, early and in the room, is the part of the job that cannot be delegated.
- When handing the deliverable back, name in one line the decision above that stays with the user.
