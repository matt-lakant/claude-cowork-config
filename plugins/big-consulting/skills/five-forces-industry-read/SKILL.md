# Five Forces Industry Read

## What this produces
A scored Five Forces assessment with evidence and a 3 to 5 year direction for each force, plus moves that shift the structure in the user's favor.

## Inputs to ask for
- Industry boundary: product scope and geography
- Market shares or top competitors' revenues
- The user's top supplier and customer concentration (share of spend, share of revenue)
- Margins or ROIC of public players over a full cycle
If share data is missing, estimate concentration from public revenues and say so.

## Method
1. Fix the boundary first; products or regions with different competitors get separate reads.
2. Rivalry: CR4 and HHI (sum of squared shares; above roughly 1,800 counts as highly concentrated under the 2023 US merger guidelines), market growth, fixed-cost share, exit barriers, differentiation, announced capacity.
3. Entry: scale economies, capital required, switching costs, channel access, regulation, incumbent retaliation history.
4. Suppliers: their concentration vs the industry's, switching costs, input share of total cost, forward-integration threat.
5. Buyers: concentration, the product's share of buyer cost, price sensitivity, backward-integration threat, price transparency.
6. Substitutes: price-performance of alternatives doing the same job, and the buyer's cost to switch.
7. Rate each force Low, Medium, or High with its two strongest indicators and a direction. Treat government and complements as factors acting on forces, as Porter does.
8. Test the read against industry ROIC vs cost of capital. If they disagree, revisit the ratings.
9. Name 2 to 4 moves that shift a force (switching costs, supplier dual-sourcing, channel ownership, consolidation).

## Output format
- Boundary statement (two sentences)
- Table: Force | Rating | Direction | Indicators with values | Source
- Profitability tie-out (under 120 words)
- Moves table: Move | Force shifted | Mechanism | Feasibility

## Quality checks
- Every rating cites a number or named fact
- Ratings reconcile with observed industry returns
- Substitutes are defined by the customer's job
- Every force has a direction of change

## Notes from the source article
- **Replaces:** The industry attractiveness module of a strategy review: a team gathers concentration, supplier, buyer, and entry-barrier data, then argues for two days over whether each force is high or medium.
- **Example request:** “Run a five forces read on regional less-than-truckload freight in the Southeast. Our top ten customers are 40% of revenue, and I want to know how much of our margin squeeze is structural.”
- **What the user still owns:** The call on whether the structure is yours to change or a condition to price into the plan, and saying so plainly to the board.
- When handing the deliverable back, name in one line the decision above that stays with the user.
