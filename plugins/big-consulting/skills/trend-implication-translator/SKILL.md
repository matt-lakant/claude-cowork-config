# Trend-to-Implication Translator

## What this produces
A one-page trend register tracing every surviving trend to a P&L line, with size range, timing, certainty, and response.

## Inputs to ask for
- Trend material: reports, articles, notes (attach PDFs or paste)
- The user's P&L by line item, or revenue by segment and major cost lines
- Planning horizon (default 5 years)
- Trends leadership has already debated
Without trend material, generate candidates across political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal categories and mark each as needing validation.

## Method
1. Extract and merge trends. State each as a measurable change ("share of X doing Y rising from A to B").
2. Record the evidence: data point, source, date.
3. Trace the mechanism: trend, then change in customer behavior, cost, or regulation, then the affected P&L line.
4. Size the effect as a range in dollars or margin points, with the assumption chain visible.
5. Rate certainty (predetermined or uncertain) and timing (under 2 years, 2 to 5, over 5).
6. Cut any trend that moves no P&L line by more than about 1% within the horizon to the appendix.
7. Assign a response: no-regret move, option (small bet that preserves the right to scale), big bet, or monitor.
8. Pass high-impact uncertain trends forward as candidate scenario axes.

## Output format
- Table: Trend | Evidence and date | Mechanism | P&L line | Size range | Timing | Certainty | Response
- No-regret moves: up to five
- Critical uncertainties for scenarios: up to four
- Appendix: cut trends, one line each

## Quality checks
- Every trend is a measurable change
- Every implication names a P&L line for this business
- Sizes show their assumption chain
- Nothing survives on novelty alone

## Notes from the source article
- **Replaces:** The megatrends pre-read for a leadership offsite, where a team condenses forty articles and reports into a page that finally says what any of it means for this company’s P&L.
- **Example request:** “Here are twenty trend reports our team collected for the offsite. Translate them into what hits our P&L as a USD 300M regional healthcare staffing firm over the next five years.”
- **What the user still owns:** Which bets leadership funds, which trends you consciously ignore, and owning that choice when a peer acts differently.
- When handing the deliverable back, name in one line the decision above that stays with the user.
