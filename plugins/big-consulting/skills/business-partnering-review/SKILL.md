# Business Partnering Review

## What this produces
A time-allocation baseline, a gap analysis between what business leaders need and what partners deliver, a redesigned role profile, and a 90-day plan.

## Inputs to ask for
- Finance partner roster with the business units they support
- Time allocation by partner across data prep, reporting, analysis, and decision support (survey template if missing)
- Stakeholder feedback, or permission to draft a five-question stakeholder survey
- Upcoming business decisions finance should support
If no data exists, draft both surveys first and stop.

## Method
1. Baseline time across four tiers: data gathering, reporting, analysis, decision support. Most troubled functions spend the majority in the first two.
2. Summarize stakeholder needs: which decisions they want help with, where finance adds value, where it slows them down.
3. Gap analysis: needed time on decision support versus actual. Identify the data prep that should move to reporting teams or automation.
4. Redesign the role: decisions supported, forums attended, analyses owned, and what the partner stops doing.
5. Assess skills per partner: commercial understanding, analytics, influence, storytelling. Rate 1 to 4 and flag gaps.
6. Set an operating rhythm: monthly business review, quarterly deep dive, pre-reads for capital and pricing decisions.

## Output format
- Time allocation table: Partner | Data prep % | Reporting % | Analysis % | Decision support %
- Stakeholder needs summary (five bullets maximum)
- Role profile: Owns | Attends | Stops doing
- Skills heat map: Partner | Skill ratings
- 90-day plan, numbered, maximum eight actions

## Quality checks
- Time allocations sum to 100% per partner
- The stop-doing list names where that work goes
- Skills ratings are labeled self-assessed or observed
